AI Usage and Impact on Students and Professionals

Conducted a quantitative and qualitative analytical project evaluating how generative AI adoption affects learning efficiency, productivity, and critical thinking habits across academic and professional groups. Cleaned, transformed, and visualized survey and behavioral data to uncover key usage trends.

288users analyzed
12AI tools tracked
4.98avg productivity score
1.62Kest. hours saved per week

The questions

  • How evenly is adoption spread across AI tools?
  • Which tools are used most for each purpose (coding, writing, studying…)?
  • Which tools save the most time each week?
  • How does daily AI usage vary across professions?

1. The raw data

The survey data was loaded into PostgreSQL (pgAdmin 4) as a raw table. It had inconsistent text, missing values, and invalid entries.

2. Cleaning with SQL

  • Standardized text: consistent casing for gender, country, and education level.
  • Fixed ages: removed text like "yrs" and converted to numbers.
  • Handled blanks: filled missing categorical values with "Unknown".
  • Restructured the table: typed columns and a primary key on UserID, producing a clean table.

3. Dashboard in Power BI

The cleaned data was exported to Excel and modeled in Power BI across two report pages.

Key findings

  • ChatGPT dominates adoption with about 42% of users, followed by Google Gemini at roughly 13%.
  • ChatGPT leads for coding and writing, the two most common purposes.
  • Average productivity score is 4.98, and AI adoption is estimated to save about 1,620 hours per week across all users.
  • Daily usage varies widely by profession, with research and AI-related roles using AI the most.
